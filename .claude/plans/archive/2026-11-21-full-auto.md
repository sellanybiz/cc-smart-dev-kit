# Full auto: the kit's only guard is the heavy-job queue

## Summary

- The kit stops asking the person anything through hooks. Seven guards go: git-guard, merge-guard,
  deploy-guard, main-folder-guard, agent-guard (with the Fable gate and the UI rule's deny),
  session-escape-guard, and user-away's blocking. job-route, the heavy-job queue, stays.
- When the person is available, Claude asks its real questions as usual. When they are away,
  Claude takes the recommended option itself, keeps working, and records each choice as a
  decision the person reads when back. Nothing is parked, nothing waits.
- Release 6.0.0. Machines get it with `cc-smart-dev-kit update`.

## Context

The person, 2026-11-21: "I am tired ... I have to wait all time in front of the Claude session.
Please drop all the guards ... the only guard our kit will support will be the heavy-job guard.
Everything will run in full auto." Rulings:

- **What goes:** every guard except job-route.
- **What stays:** the hooks that never ask the person. That means session names, the task
  list, the cost record, the update check, tier view, review-record, after-merge, and the
  "keep working" stops (keep-going, verify-gate, builder-finish, subagent-background).
- **Order:** PR #84 (5.6.1) merges first; this plan builds on top of it.
- **Questions:** "When I am available Claude asks me questions; when I am not, it goes with the
  best one."
- **Projects:** remove the projects' own asking hooks and `permissions.ask` lists. That work is
  done in each project's own session, not here.

**Trade-off, written down at the person's ruling:** with no guard, nothing stops a mistaken
`git reset --hard` in the main checkout, a force-push to `main`, an unreviewed merge, a deploy, a
write in the main folder, or a Fable or Opus-at-max launch. Claude Code's own permission mode,
and the person's own `deny` rules, are the only stops left.

## Approach

1. **The hooks.**
   - Remove the seven guards' entries from `plugin/hooks/hooks.json`, their specs from
     `src/hooks/specs.ts` (`HOOKS`), their entries in `src/entries/`, and their bundles from
     `plugin/dist/`.
   - Delete their modules and tests:
     - git-guard: `src/hooks/git-guard.ts` and its test;
     - merge-guard: `src/hooks/merge-guard.ts` and its test;
     - deploy-guard: `src/hooks/deploy-guard.ts` and its test;
     - main-folder-guard: `src/hooks/main-folder-guard.ts`;
     - agent-guard: `src/hooks/agent-guard*.ts` and `src/hooks/script-parser.ts`, so acorn
       leaves the bundles (and `package.json`, if nothing else uses it);
     - session-escape-guard: `src/hooks/session-escape-guard.ts` and its test.
   - Keep every module job-route or another kept hook still imports: the shell reader
     (`src/shell/`), `kit-invocation`, `budget`, `run.ts`, the ask log. Delete only what has no
     caller left. `tsc` and the tests decide this, not a guess.
   - Move the seven names to `RETIRED_HOOK_NAMES` (`src/project/config.ts`). An `ownHooks` that
     names one still parses and silences nothing, and `doctor` says the name can go.
   - `entries.test.ts` and `matrix.test.ts` list the new set.
2. **user-away.** The `user` command, its state and the user-available skill stay.
   - **Question tools:** while the person is away, the hook still refuses `AskUserQuestion` and
     `ExitPlanMode`. Its words change: "The person is away: take the recommended option yourself,
     keep working, and record it with `cc-smart-dev-kit user park --kind decision` ('Summary: …')."
     No more parking of questions or actions.
   - **The frame:** `run.ts` step 5, which turned another guard's ask into a deny plus a parked
     action, is removed. No guard asks any more; job-route never asks.
   - **PermissionRequest** (Claude Code's own permission dialog): while away, the hook answers
     nothing and lets Claude Code show its dialog. A hook never approves for the person.
   - **The Stop part** (`user-away-stop.ts`) stays: it tells a session once that the person is
     away.
3. **The instructions and skills** (`plugin/`). Say what is true now, one home each:
   - `instructions/core.md` § Subagents loses "`opus` at `max` and any `fable` launch ask the
     person", which the guard enforced. The rule becomes the instruction "ask the person before a
     `fable` or `opus`-at-`max` launch when they are available; when away, use `opus` at `xhigh`".
     The UI/UX rule stays as an instruction, no longer enforced.
   - **user-unavailable:** away means decide with the recommended option, record a decision,
     keep going; never park, never wait. **user-available:** shows the decisions.
   - **commit, build, review, pull-request, tasks, worktree, subagent-decision:** remove "the
     guard asks" wording. The workflow settings say what Claude does with no prompt, and
     `autoMerge` merges once CI is green and the review is recorded, as an instruction.
   - **The agents** (senior-engineer, auditor, edge-case-hunter), `instructions/optin/worktrees.md`
     and the two workflow scripts: their guard wording.
4. **The CLI and portal.** `doctor`'s guard lines go. Each name is printed once as retired. The
   portal's guard-asks view stays readable for old records and shows no new ones. `setup` installs
   nothing new.
5. **Docs.** `docs/design/hooks.md` (the guard rows and the fail-safe table, now job-route only),
   `docs/design.md`'s index, `README.md` (the hooks table and the commands), `.claude/CLAUDE.md`'s
   rule table (`hooks-guards.md`: it shrinks to job-route and the frame), `docs/claude-code-contract.md`
   (rows that only a removed guard relied on move to a retired list), and `CHANGELOG.md` 6.0.0 with
   **What to run:** `cc-smart-dev-kit update`, then restart open sessions.
6. **The queued plans.**
   - **#616** (planning-model guard) is a guard: it is dropped. The person's rule (planning
     subagents on `opus` at `xhigh`, Fable with their yes) becomes the instruction above, in
     subagent-decision and plan-task, in this plan.
   - **#631** (allow-workflow) loses its merge-guard parts. It keeps the `allow*` rename, the
     no-`--auto` instruction, the hand-back commit check and the job-queue `tsc` bug. It runs after
     this plan.
7. **Version:** 6.0.0 in `plugin.json`, `install.sh`, `install.ps1`; rebuild `plugin/dist`.
   `router.js` must not change.

The main session does the version, `dist`, the two review rounds (the build skill § 6, risky:
it removes the safety layer), the final review only if the rule calls for it, and the full check.

## Edge cases

- **An old `ownHooks` naming a removed guard** still parses; `doctor` says it can go.
- **A machine updating from 5.6.x** with sessions open keeps the old guards until the session
  restarts (the What to run line).
- **job-route keeps working:** heavy commands are still queued, and its refusal of a heavy line
  mixed with other commands stays. It is a deny, not a question to the person.
- **Claude Code's own isolation check** (a worktree session refusing complex git lines) is Claude
  Code's, not the kit's: it stays.
- **A project's own hooks** (the person's other projects) are theirs; this plan does not touch
  them.

## How we prove it

- Tests: `entries.test.ts` and `matrix.test.ts` list exactly the kept hooks. A row proves
  `hooks.json` wires no removed guard. A row proves an `ownHooks` with a removed name parses. The
  user-away rows prove an away `AskUserQuestion` is refused with the new words and nothing is
  parked.
- Checks: `bun run check`, both validates, portal tests, `build:check` (`router.js` unchanged).
- Live, after the update: a `git reset --hard`, a `gh pr merge` and an `Agent` launch on `fable`
  all run with no hook question; `bun test` is still queued.

## Rollback

A version number never goes down (`.claude/rules/bundles-and-releases.md`), and the self-update
would undo a reinstall of 5.6.1. The way back is a new version: revert the pull request, rebuild
`plugin/dist` and release 6.0.1 with the guards back. Machines get it with `cc-smart-dev-kit
update`. Nothing is stored or migrated.
