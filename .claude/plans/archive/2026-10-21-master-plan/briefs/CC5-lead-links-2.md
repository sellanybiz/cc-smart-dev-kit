# CC5 — leads of one repo reach each other

**Goal.** Several leads often work in one repo at once (about 11 in the owner's biggest project). Today each lead is its own hub: a crew session tells only the crew of its own lead (`src/crew/tell.ts:86`), nothing tells a lead when another lead's phase claims the same files, and a merge into the base reaches the other sessions only as a message the merging session must send by hand. The owner chose (2026-10-23) to add all three parts below.

## How to read

## Build
1. **`lead tell`: one lead to another.** A lead sends a note to one or more other leads of the same repo, in the two kinds `crew tell` has (`contract`, `question`). Copy `crew tell`'s shape: the store decides who may be told (a live lead of this repo, from `listLeads`, `src/crew/store.ts:851`); the words have one home (`src/crew/tell-format.ts`); all or none. The note is recorded in the store's events (`appendCrewEvent`, :885), so the receiving lead's own watch prints one line (`src/crew/watch.ts`) and its `lead-notes` hook covers a missed one. Pick the command's name to fit the existing crew command and its usage text (`src/crew/command.ts`, `usage.ts`). A crew session may not use it: it talks to its own lead.
2. **A file clash warning at `crew start`.** When the new phase's `--files` overlap the files of a running or queued crew session of **another** lead in this repo, `crew start` still starts or queues it, and prints one line naming the other lead, its crew session and the files they share. The other lead's watch gets one line too. Decide overlap on real paths: expand both glob lists against the repo's tracked files, and treat two globs that match no file yet as overlapping when one's fixed prefix contains the other's. Never block on it: two leads may mean to share a file.
3. **A merge into the base reaches the other leads.** `after-merge` (`src/hooks/after-merge.ts`, the note at lines 10–15) already writes the message for every other session of the repo when a pull request merges into `worktrees.base`. Change who it names: the other leads and the person's plain sessions, never a crew session (a crew session follows its own lead's branch, and merging the base into it would be wrong). Also record one event so each other lead's watch prints the line without waiting for a message.
4. **The lead skill** (`plugin/skills/lead/SKILL.md`): one row in § 4's table for each new line (a note from another lead, a file clash), and one line in § 3 on the clash warning. **The crew skill** says nothing new: a crew session never talks to another lead.
